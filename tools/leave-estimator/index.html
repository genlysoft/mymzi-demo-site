Three clocks, statutory floor

illustrative · calendar days
Pay
When the paycheck changes
Accumulated sick leave (30 days)
—
Full pay until accumulated sick leave exhausts, counted in duty days.
Differential pay — five school months
—
Salary minus the substitute's cost, once per illness, once per fiscal year — under the § 44977 model.
A § 44983 district instead pays 50% from day eleven, concurrent with sick leave. Same question, different arithmetic — selected by district policy.
Job protection
When the right to return ends
FMLA / CFRA — 12 workweeks, concurrent
—
Twelve workweeks of job protection in a 12-month period, counted in duty days: school breaks do not consume the entitlement.
