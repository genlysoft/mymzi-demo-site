The Decoder

Every acronym in the room, in plain English — and the tool or desk it points at. Plain-English summaries, not legal advice.

Free · statute only

FMLA

Family and Medical Leave Act

Up to 12 weeks per year of unpaid, job-protected leave. Protects the job, not the paycheck — pay and protection are separate clocks.

→ Leave Estimator

CFRA

California Family Rights Act

California's FMLA counterpart. Overlaps but is not identical (it covers a “designated person”), so the two sometimes run concurrently and sometimes don't.

→ Leave Estimator

PDL

Pregnancy Disability Leave

A separate California entitlement of up to four months that runs before CFRA bonding leave — a pregnancy can lawfully stack three leave clocks.

Designation notice

The written notice saying “this absence counts against your FMLA.” If nobody sends it, the 12 weeks may never legally start running.

→ Leave Compass

Differential pay

Ed Code § 44977

After a teacher exhausts sick leave, roughly five school months of pay at salary minus the substitute's cost. Starting the clock late is exactly the dispute Leave Compass exists to prevent.

Duty day

A day the employee is scheduled to work. Leave is counted in duty days, so school breaks do not consume the 12 workweeks — though a holiday inside a leave week does.

CBA

Collective bargaining agreement

The union contract: pay, hours, leave, transfers, seniority, discipline procedure. Per-district and per-unit, which is why no vendor ships these rules pre-built.

→ Connect your contract

Grievance

The contract's built-in dispute process, on strict timelines, usually ending in arbitration. Missing a step deadline can forfeit the case outright.

RIF

Reduction in force

A layoff. In California schools it runs on statutory notice dates, seniority tie-breakers, and bumping rules — annual in lean years, entirely manual today.

→ March 15 Countdown

March 15 notice

The deadline to warn certificated employees they may be laid off next year. Miss it and the employee is employed for another year, period.

Bumping

In a layoff, a senior employee displaces a junior one in an equal or lower class. One layoff can trigger a chain HR models by hand.

Skelly

Before disciplining a permanent public employee, California requires notice of the charges, the evidence, and a chance to respond. Skipping it voids the discipline.

→ Skelly Prep

Just cause / the seven tests

The arbitration standard for whether discipline sticks: fair notice, reasonable rule, real investigation, fair investigation, proof, even-handed treatment, proportional penalty.

Certificated

Credentialed school staff: teachers, counselors, administrators. “Certificated,” not “certified” — the California term of art.

Classified

School employees whose jobs don't require a credential: aides, secretaries, custodians, bus drivers. Their layoff and leave rules differ from teachers' — the divergence is the point.

UCP

Uniform Complaint Procedures

California's standardized process for complaints alleging discrimination or program violations: a 60-calendar-day clock and a written decision, appealable to CDE within 30 days.

→ District Clock

Williams complaint

Ed Code § 35186

A special lane for insufficient textbooks, facility problems, and teacher vacancies — 30 working days, with the working-day math depending on the calendar.

→ District Clock

Title IX

The federal ban on sex discrimination in federally funded education. Its regulations prescribe a deadline-driven process; get it wrong and the outcome is voidable.

CPRA

California Public Records Act

The public's right to inspect government records, with a 10-day clock to respond and a body of exemptions.

→ District Clock

Brown Act

California's open-meeting law: agendas posted 72 hours ahead, no action on off-agenda items. Violations can void board action.

IEP

Individualized education program

The legally binding plan for a student with disabilities. Procedural defects are what parents' attorneys hunt for — and what IEP Shield flags first.

MDR

Manifestation determination review

Before expelling a student with an IEP, the team must decide whether the behavior was a manifestation of the disability. If it was, discipline changes course.

CalSAAS

The state system that audits teacher assignments against credentials annually. Districts prepare by hand-reconciling master schedules.

→ Credential Checker

30-day sub permit

An emergency permit letting a substitute cover any class — but only briefly per assignment. Day 31 is a misassignment.

COE

County Office of Education

California's 58 county agencies: oversight, credential monitoring, shared services including payroll. One relationship reaches 10–40 districts.

Position control

The budgeting ledger tying every employee to an authorized, funded position. No open position, no hire.

Rules-as-code

Turning governing documents — CBAs, statutes, board policy — into executable, citable rules a system can apply and a human can audit. The layer nobody owns.
