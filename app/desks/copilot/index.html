Question · Principal, Mesa Vista Middle

Can we deny a voluntary transfer request from a teacher with four years' seniority when a less senior teacher is a better fit for the EL cohort?

Answer, grounded
every claim traces to a retrieved clause

Under your contract, a voluntary transfer goes to the most senior qualified applicant unless the district documents a specific programmatic need — and “qualified” is defined by credential authorization, not fit. If the EL cohort requires an EL authorization the senior applicant lacks, that applicant is not qualified under Article 12.3.1 and the denial is consistent with the contract. If both hold the authorization, seniority controls and a denial would likely be grievable under Article 18. This is a contract question; whether the programmatic-need exception applies on these facts is a judgment for HR and counsel.

Source panel — the actual text
CBA Article 12.3.1 — Voluntary transfers
Mesa Vista USD–MVTA CBA 2024–27, Art. 12.3.1

“Voluntary transfer requests shall be granted to the most senior qualified applicant. “Qualified” means holding the credential and authorizations required for the position. The District may deny a request upon written statement of a specific programmatic need, provided to the unit member and the Association within ten (10) days.”
